ORDINA Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 App: ORDINA (Android, package com.ordina.app) Developer: Enoveta Contact: enoveta.inc@gmail.com

1. What ORDINA does

ORDINA helps you plan your day. You create tasks yourself, or describe your day to ORDINA AI (by typing, speaking, or attaching a photo or file) and it proposes a plan that you review, edit and confirm before anything is saved.

2. Data stored on your phone only

ORDINA has no ORDINA server. Everything below stays on your phone. We (the developer) never receive it.

Data Why
Account: name, email address, 4-digit passkey (stored only as a hash, in the phone's secure storage) Signing in and locking the app
Tasks: title, description, date, time, duration, priority, category, status The core app feature
ORDINA AI chats and the photos or files you attached to them Showing your chat history (you choose how long it is kept)
Planning habits learned from your own tasks Making future plans match your habits
Settings: language, theme, categories, notification choices Your preferences

3. AI processing by Google Gemini

When you use ORDINA AI, your phone sends the request directly to the Google Gemini API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Only what is needed for that request is sent:

  • the text you type, or the voice recording you make (for transcription);
  • photos, videos or files (PDF, Word, TXT, CSV) you choose to attach;
  • today's date, your local time and time zone;
  • your existing tasks (titles, dates, times, durations, priority and status), so the plan avoids clashes;
  • the recent messages of the chat you are in.

Nothing is sent to Google unless you use ORDINA AI.

ORDINA AI currently uses the free tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms for the free tier (Unpaid Services), Google may use the content sent and the responses to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Please do not put sensitive personal information (such as health, financial or identity details) in AI requests. See https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. This policy will be updated if ORDINA moves to Google's paid tier.

4. Permissions

Permission Used for When it is asked
Microphone Speaking a request to ORDINA AI The first time you tap the microphone
Camera, photos and videos Attaching a picture to ORDINA AI The first time you attach one
Notifications, exact alarms, start after restart Task reminders on time When you turn reminders on

ORDINA does not use your location, contacts or calendar in this version. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or share your data.

5. Backups

Settings → Backup creates a file with your tasks that you can save or share wherever you choose. ORDINA never uploads it anywhere itself. The AI key is never included.

6. Security

Your passkey is stored only as a hash in the phone's secure storage. Requests to Google Gemini use HTTPS.

7. How long data is kept, and deleting it

Data stays on your phone until you delete it:

  • Delete your account and all its data: Settings → Delete account (account, passkey, tasks and ORDINA AI chats).
  • Only tasks: Settings → Delete all tasks on this phone. Only chats: Settings → Delete all chats.
  • Uninstalling ORDINA removes all of its data from the phone.

Data already processed by Google Gemini is kept according to Google's terms (section 3).

8. Children

ORDINA is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data use changes and change the effective date above.
